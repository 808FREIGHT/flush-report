# Pua Pump Station / Puhi Bay Image Research Notes

I searched for real visual references using the queries “Pua Pump Station Hilo,” “Pua Pump Station Puhi Bay,” and “County Hawaii Pua Pump Station.” The strongest image result is a Big Island Video News map-style visual titled “Wastewater Discharge Reported In Hilo's Puhi Bay,” which labels the Pua Sewage Pump Station and the ocean outfall route. This is contextually accurate, but it is not a photographic hero image.

The Civil Beat source confirms the Puhi Bay wastewater context: a 607,000-gallon release entered Puhi Bay in June 2024, and the article states that the Hilo plant discharges treated wastewater through an outfall and diffuser system 4,500 feet offshore in Puhi Bay, east of the Hilo breakwater. It also includes a real image captioned “The Hilo wastewater treatment plant has corroded equipment that has exceeded its life expectancy. (Hawaii County)” and another Puhi Bay/Hilo Bay advisory visual.

The County of Hawaii project page confirms the Hilo Wastewater Treatment Plant Rehabilitation and Replacement Project and describes planned improvements, including replacement of on-site pump stations, effluent disinfection upgrades, and instrumentation/control improvements.

The Big Island Now article provides real 2025 Hilo Wastewater Treatment Plant photographs by Kelsey Walling, including plant tour imagery, cleaned water flowing out of the plant after effluent disinfection, and corrosion/rust at secondary clarifiers. This appears to be the best photographic source if an exact public Pua Pump Station + Puhi Bay background image is unavailable.

## Visual Candidate Inspection

The official County courtesy Hilo Wastewater Treatment Plant image shows the treatment facility in daylight with mountain/landscape background. It is a credible real infrastructure visual and was associated with Big Island Video News coverage of the Hilo Wastewater Treatment Plant emergency proclamation, credited as courtesy of the County of Hawaiʻi.

The Hilo wastewater outfall pipe image shows a real shoreline pipe extending through lava rock into coastal water, which is visually closer to the user’s requested discharge-pipe and Puhi Bay story context. The source page identifies it as a file image of the Hilo Wastewater Treatment Plant outfall pipe from video recorded in 2016 and states that the concrete pipe discharges secondary disinfected treated effluent 4,400 feet from the shoreline at Puhi Bay in Keaukaha. This is likely the strongest visual replacement for the Keaukaha origin card because it directly ties the user-facing story to the ocean/outfall context, even though it is not a full Pua Pump Station facility portrait.
