## Selected Direction

I am selecting **Neo-Broadcast Brutalism** because it best balances the user’s goals: clean and simple, professional and TV-news-inspired, viral and witty, but still credible enough for sponsors, municipalities, operators, and community members. It also protects the brand legally and strategically by focusing the message on **user behavior, taxpayer waste, infrastructure stress, landfill burden, and water-quality impact**, rather than shaming wipe manufacturers or naming corporate targets.

The site will therefore use a dark newsroom base, Colts Blue civic accents, sparing alert red, compressed broadcast headlines, clear field-report panels, a persistent ticker, a strong “File Your Report” call to action, and a “Cost of the Clog” section that explains how money spent fixing preventable damage could be put to better community use.

When implementing every page, component, and stylesheet, the guiding question is: **Does this choice reinforce or dilute the Neo-Broadcast Brutalism philosophy?**
