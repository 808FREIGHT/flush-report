# Ick-Factor Image Selection Notes

The contact sheet shows nine converted field images from the uploaded HEIC files. The strongest educational image is `pasted_file_pK6UJ1_IMG_7636.jpg`, which clearly shows a mass of wipes, trash, and debris removed from wastewater infrastructure. It has the most immediate “this should not be flushed” impact, while still being understandable and useful for public education.

The second row and top-row pipe/cleanout images provide useful operator-process context, but several are visually repetitive or less legible at website scale. The final site should use the strongest debris image as the main “ick factor” proof point, with one or two supporting images only if they improve the story without making the page gratuitous.

Recommended use: place the main debris photo in the new educational section or near the cost/flow explanation with a short caption such as “This is what ‘flushable’ can become after it leaves the bathroom.” Keep the tone worker-respectful and public-awareness focused.

Current recommendation: use `pasted_file_pK6UJ1_IMG_7636.jpg` as the main “ick factor” proof image and `pasted_file_qUBumC_IMG_7900.jpg` as a secondary operator-action image if the layout can support it without becoming visually heavy. Avoid using too many gross images at once.
