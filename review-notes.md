# The Flush Report — Coastal Revision Review Notes

The revised page now reads as a brighter coastal public-service news site rather than a dark futuristic newsroom. The background is light ocean blue, the page uses professional white editorial cards, and the sea-glass accent color makes the site feel more tied to water, community, and Hawaii without losing the broadcast structure.

The hero headline has been softened from “Stop Flushing That.” to “Think Before You Flush.” This preserves the consumer-accountability message while feeling less aggressive, more sponsor-safe, and more educational. The lead paragraph now explains that what goes down the toilet does not simply disappear, connecting daily behavior to infrastructure, tax money, operator work, and water quality.

The page still keeps the news feel through the fixed header, field bulletin ticker, lower-third labels, editorial sections, File Your Report intake, FRTV programming blocks, and sponsor positioning. The overall result feels cleaner, warmer, and more soulful while still being clear enough for a media brand and public-awareness campaign.

Validation completed: TypeScript checking passed, and the production build completed successfully after the coastal redesign and headline adjustment. The MVP form remains a static front-end demo; the next technical step would be full-stack file upload and report review workflow if real submissions are needed.
